About LABGUARD

Empowering educational institutions and corporate enterprises with robust Windows endpoint protection and laboratory management.

Secure Labs • Smarter Tomorrow

LABGUARD – System Protection is a premier Windows application engineered specifically for secure, controlled computer laboratories. In environments where dozens or hundreds of users access workstations daily, maintaining system integrity, preventing unauthorized software execution, and protecting sensitive configurations is paramount.

Our advanced kernel-level protection and centralized management console allow administrators to lock down endpoints, monitor live sessions, manage authorized user privileges, and ensure compliance across all lab facilities with zero friction.
